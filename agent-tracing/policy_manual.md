# Support policy manual

## 1. Returns

An item can be returned within 30 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original box. Opened software, gift cards and custom-built computers cannot be returned. The customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or was not what was ordered.

Examples for section 1: a customer asking about returns should be given the rule above in one or two sentences, the next step they need to take, and nothing else. If the rule does not cover the case, say so and offer to hand over to a colleague.

## 2. Refunds

A refund goes to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving at the warehouse. Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is our fault. Store credit can be offered instead and is issued the same day.

## 3. Faulty items

An item that stops working within 12 months of delivery is repaired or replaced at no cost. Ask for the order number, a description of the fault and a photo. Do not ask the customer to open the device.

## 4. Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and express takes 1 to 2. A parcel can be traced with the carrier after day 8. Orders over 60 dollars ship free on standard.

## 5. Lost parcels

A parcel is treated as lost when tracking has not changed for 10 days. Offer a replacement or a refund, at the customer's choice, and open a claim with the carrier.

## 6. Cancellations

An order can be cancelled until it is handed to the carrier. After that it has to be delivered and returned. A cancelled order is refunded within 3 business days.

## 7. Price matching

We match the price of an identical item sold by an authorised retailer in the same country, before purchase or within 14 days after. Marketplace sellers and clearance sales are excluded.

## 8. Warranty extensions
